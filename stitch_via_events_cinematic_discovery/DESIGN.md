## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a luxury cinematic editorial language tailored for an elite cultural curation platform. Evoking the atmospheric mystery of coastal Mediterranean nights, ancient stone amphitheaters, and forward-leaning art gatherings under deep open skies, the visual space avoids generic enterprise tropes and flat SaaS interfaces.

The emotional baseline is immersive, mysterious, prestigious, and intensely curated. The UI behaves like high-production cinematography: deliberate, atmospheric, layered, and illuminated by targeted spectral light sources rather than flat ambient illumination. The aesthetic synthesizes:
- **Atmospheric Nocturne:** Abyssal midnight depths acting as a spatial canvas rather than a flat background.
- **Editorial Film Poster Layouts:** Edge-bleeding typography, asymmetrical mastheads, and panoramic visual frames reminiscent of premium art monograph publishing.
- **Spectral Luminescence:** Subtle starlight gradients, soft-diffuse colored flares, and translucent celestial glass surfaces that react dynamically to cursor proximity and event category palettes.

## Colors
The palette is built strictly on pitch-dark midnight tones illuminated by radiant, chromatic emissive colors.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#090A14`):** The primordial cosmic void. Serves as the global backdrop, accented by film grain and distant chromatic vignette gradients.
- **Surface Layer (`#111322`):** Primary paneling and background tier for layered content panels.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#16192E`):** Distinct interactive planes, dialog overlays, and navigation rails.

### Celestial Emitters
- **Primary Luminescence — Cosmic Violet (`#7C5CFF`):** Central focal points, primary interactive states, prestige tags, and main cursor-following flares.
- **Secondary Luminescence — Electric Magenta (`#FF3D9A`):** Nightlife, high-energy live performance, headline accentuation, and active ticket triggers.
- **Tertiary Accent — Coastal Cyan (`#22D3EE`):** Mediterranean seaside venues, curated architectural exhibitions, dates, and precision telemetry metrics.
- **Auxiliary Accent — Warm Amber (`#FFB547`):** Classical heritage, acoustic performances, ancient archaeological sites, VIP distinctions, and golden hour markers.

### Contrast & Legibility
Text layers leverage high optical clarity against midnight tones: pure starlight white (`#F8FAFC`) for editorial headings, soft lunar silver (`#94A3B8`) for body prose, and muted slate (`#475569`) for subtle metadata and structural constellation guides.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy creates a high-tension dialogue between dramatic Italianate classical styling and cold, razor-sharp technical modernism.

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Reserved for dramatic statements, event titles, curators' notes, and high-impact theatrical pull quotes. Utilizes tight negative tracking at large scale with italicized accents to evoke high-end editorial and cinematic title design.
- **Technical UI Grotesque (Space Grotesk):** Deployed for temporal data, coordinates, venue locations, ticket matrices, and structured metadata. The geometric proportion and deliberate letterforms balance the lyrical flourish of the serif display with engineered architectural precision.
- **Case Rule:** Micro-labels, categorical taxonomies, and geographic markers are consistently set in uppercase with tracked spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.18em`) to enforce structural order.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an asymmetrical editorial canvas model designed to mirror luxury exhibition catalogues and spatial stage compositions rather than uniform digital dashboards.

### Grid & Composition
- **Constellation Grid:** A 12-column layout governed by dynamic anchor lines. Columns intentionally carry wide horizontal air (`gutter-desktop: 2.5rem`) allowing cinematic imagery and type to interlock.
- **Bleed & Overlap:** Feature event showcases break outside structural containers, bleeding onto screen boundaries while technical telemetry (coordinates, dates, venue IDs) rests anchored along fine alignment threads.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Generous rhythmic pacing allows editorial moments to breathe, transitioning from tight typographic groupings directly into expansive, cosmic negative space (`space-xl`).

### Responsive Adaptation
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Asymmetrical split-screens, poster overlaps, floating technical sidebar pins, and edge-to-edge ambient stage lighting.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** Content compresses to an 8-column layout. Multi-layered poster compositions collapse into sequenced horizontal galleries with fixed typographic anchors.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Reflows into a single dramatic column. Hero type adjusts scale dynamically, cards expand to fluid viewport margins (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`), and navigation transforms into a minimal bottom docking constellation rail.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed through spatial darkfield layering and atmospheric illumination rather than traditional drop shadows.

- **Atmospheric Layering:** Depth is conveyed by transitioning through tonal tiers: base canvas (`#090A14`), middle spatial surface (`#111322`), and top interactive layer (`#16192E`).
- **Celestial Backlight & Halos:** Elevated components cast ultra-soft, chromatic glows back into the midnight canvas. Focus and hover states emit diffused radiance (blur radius 40px–60px, 15% opacity in `#7C5CFF` or `#FF3D9A`), mimicking stage spotlights casting through coastal haze.
- **Glass & Membrane Translucency:** Floating panels utilize layered backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`) over 60–80% opacity dark bases, bound by hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with illuminated top-edge specular highlights (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2), transparent)`).
- **Film Grain Atmosphere:** A subtle procedural SVG noise filter overlay sits continuously above background tiers at 2.5% opacity, dissolving digital banding into cinematic film emulsion.

## Shapes
The structural language leans into architectural precision, drawing inspiration from classical monuments, brutalist modern stone pavilions, and framed fine-art prints.

- **Crisp Geometry with Softened Edges:** UI containers, event frames, and interactive fields employ minimal border radius (`0.25rem`), keeping contours sharp and monolithic without being aggressively harsh.
- **Architectural Framing:** Image frames and focal areas utilize micro-notches, fine crosshairs, and delicate outer bounding frames that feel like optical alignment viewfinders.
- **Pill Exception for Micro-Tags:** High-visibility contextual badges, category identifiers, and soundscape status indicators adopt fully curved pill silhouettes to visually differentiate status data from architectural event containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Celestial Action:** High-saturation Violet (`#7C5CFF`) base with crisp white Space Grotesk typography. On hover, the button initiates a subtle expansion, emitting an Electric Magenta and Violet back-glow.
- **Cinematic Outline:** Hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`) across a dark translucent plane (`rgba(17, 19, 34, 0.5)`). Transitions to solid white text and intense specular border sheen on hover.
- **Text Link with Star Ray:** Editorial text accompanied by an acute hairline directional arrow, tracked broadly with micro-offset on active hover.

### Event Showcase Surfaces (Cards)
- **Zero-SaaS Aesthetic:** Generic white boxes and drop shadows are strictly prohibited.
- **Poster Bleed Composition:** Composed of layered midnight glass (`rgba(22, 25, 46, 0.75)`), containing full-bleed dramatic photography treated with a subtle dual-tone oceanic grade.
- **Information Stacking:** Date, venue, and geographical coordinates occupy the top perimeter in tracked technical typography; title spans the lower third in Playfair Display serif, breaking seamlessly across imagery.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Darkfield Inset:** Inputs sit recessed in `#090A14` with a resting border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Focus Activation:** Glowing hairline border illuminates with Coastal Cyan (`#22D3EE`) or Cosmic Violet (`#7C5CFF`), accompanied by an ultra-subtle 12px outer field illumination.
- **Placeholders:** Styled in soft lunar slate (`#94A3B8`) with Space Grotesk letterforms.

### Status Chips & Category Badges
- **Pill Silhouette:** Dark translucent core (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with micro-borders tinted in corresponding category light: Amber (`#FFB547`) for Heritage/Classical, Cyan (`#22D3EE`) for Mediterranean Arts, and Magenta (`#FF3D9A`) for Night Electronic/Avant-Garde.
- **Telemetry Indicators:** Incorporate a pulsing 4px luminescent dot at the leading edge.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Monolithic Squares:** Precise geometric checkboxes (`rounded: 2px`) styled with high-contrast starlight checkmarks inside an illuminated Cyan glow border on selection.
- **Segmented Cultural Switchers:** Dark recessed tracks housing smooth-sliding translucent glass sliders that snap between curated cultural event modes.

### Atmospheric Navigation Bar
- **Constellation Masthead:** Floating horizontal glass membrane anchored to the top viewport with 32px backdrop blur, featuring fine editorial typography, an illuminated monogram mark, and a streamlined audio soundscape toggle.
